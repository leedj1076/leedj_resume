I said so upfront, then learned by working directly with developers, attending Unity Professional Services sessions, and building the photogrammetry pipeline hands-on. Pretending to know something you don't is the fastest way to lose credibility — with founders, with partners, and with your own team."

## 9. 알토스 포트폴리오 & 투자 시뮬레이션 (Portfolio & Investment Simulation)

### Q9.1: 알토스 포트폴리오 중 가장 관심 있는 회사가 있나요? 왜요?
**Why they ask:** Shows you've done your homework and can think analytically about existing investments.

**Answer Direction:** Pick one you genuinely find interesting and can speak intelligently about. Research it beforehand. Connect to your experience — e.g., if you pick a gaming/XR company, leverage your DUG experience. If you pick an AI company, leverage your RAG/GNN experience.

---

### Q9.2: [시뮬레이션] 이 회사의 피칭 덱을 보고 평가해주세요.
**Why they ask:** This may come as a case study. The @bakisalon/43 intern role specifically includes 투자검토업무 서포트 and analyzing 산업의 크기, 시장 동향, 경쟁 상황.

**Answer Direction:** Prepare a framework: (1) Team — founder's domain expertise and track record, (2) Market — TAM/SAM/SOM, growth rate, timing, (3) Product — differentiation, user traction, retention metrics, (4) Business model — unit economics, scalability, capital efficiency, (5) Competition — moat analysis, competitive response scenarios, (6) Risk factors — what could go wrong and how likely, (7) Altos fit — does this compound over 10+ years?

---

### Q9.3: 투자 메모를 작성해본 적이 있나요? 어떻게 작성하시겠어요?
**Why they ask:** Writing investment memos is a core job function.

**Answer Direction:** "While I haven't written a formal VC investment memo, I've written analogous documents: investor pitch materials at Flint, competitive analysis reports at TmaxTibero, and partnership evaluation memos at DUG (the Meta Quest+ entry analysis was essentially an investment memo — evaluating risk, modeling revenue scenarios, and making a go/no-go recommendation). For a proper VC memo, I'd structure it as: Executive Summary, Founder/Team Assessment, Market Analysis, Product & Technology Evaluation, Financial Model & Projections, Competitive Landscape, Key Risks & Mitigants, and Investment Recommendation with Terms."

## 10. 상황 판단 & 케이스 (Situational Judgment & Cases)

### Q10.1: 포트폴리오 회사 대표가 '우리 회사 상황이 안 좋다'고 전화했습니다. 어떻게 하시겠어요?
**Why they ask:** Testing operational empathy and crisis management instinct.

**Answer Direction:** "First, listen. Understand the specific nature of the crisis — is it a runway problem, a key person leaving, a customer churn spike, or a product failure? Each requires a different response. Then, mobilize the right Altos resources — if it's a hiring crisis, connect them with the recruiting team; if it's legal, loop in legal support. But most importantly, be honest about what I can and can't help with, and maintain the founder's confidence. Having been a founder in tough situations, I know the worst thing an investor can do is panic or go silent. Show up, be calm, be practical."

---

### Q10.2: 공동 투자 중인 다른 VC가 우리와 다른 의견을 가지고 있으면 어떻게 하시겠어요?
**Why they ask:** Co-investor management is a real VC skill.

**Answer Direction:** "Seek to understand their reasoning first. Different VCs have different time horizons, fund sizes, and portfolio strategies — a difference in opinion often stems from different incentive structures, not different intelligence. If the disagreement is about a strategic direction for the portfolio company, the founder's vision should be the primary guide, with both VCs providing data and perspective rather than directives. If it's about follow-on investment decisions, present my analysis transparently and let the data drive the conversation. Altos' reputation as the most trusted VC in Korea gives it significant soft power in these discussions."

---

### Q10.3: 한 달에 100개의 스타트업 피칭을 듣는다면, 어떻게 효율적으로 필터링하시겠어요?
**Why they ask:** Deal flow management is essential for associate-level productivity.

**Answer Direction:** "Stage 1 — Quick filter (5 minutes per company): Market size sufficient? Founder background relevant to the problem? Revenue/traction at appropriate stage? This eliminates 70%. Stage 2 — Deep read (30 minutes): Full pitch deck review, competitive analysis, initial founder research. This narrows to 10-15. Stage 3 — Founder conversation (1 hour): The most important filter. Do they deeply understand their problem? Are they honest about what they don't know? Do I want to work with them for 10+ years? This is the Altos filter — you can't evaluate founder quality from a deck. Stage 4 — Full due diligence for the final 2-3."

**Why they ask:** Understanding your motivations and whether they're aligned with what Altos offers.

(2) Exposure to a diverse portfolio of companies across stages and sectors — this is the fastest way to develop pattern recognition.
