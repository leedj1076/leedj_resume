Q: How much did you raise at Flint Technologies, from whom, and what was the single hardest moment in the fundraising process?

A: Strong Ventures and Fast Ventures invested $212K in our Seed Round at a $2.127M post-money valuation, for a 10% equity stake (preferred stock). Later we secured a KRW 200 million bank loan with a guarantee from KODIT. The hardest moment was convincing VCs to invest on our terms despite two unfavorable conditions: (1) US incorporation — Korean VCs disliked the bureaucracy of overseas investment reporting, and we had several meetings where they tried to convince us not to incorporate in the US. (2) Equal 25% equity split among all 4 founders — VCs in Korea don't prefer this structure. We held firm on both because US incorporation was strategically necessary and equal equity was the fair thing to do since all founders were fully committed.
